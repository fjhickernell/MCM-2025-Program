\begin{itemize}

\section{Guidance for Speakers and Session Chairs}

\subsection{Speakers}

Thank you for sharing with us your latest insight.  Be aware that your audience will likely include experts as well as the curious.  Please make the first one-third of your talk accessible to a general audience.
\begin{itemize}
	\item Plenary speakers please keep your talk to \textbf{50 minutes} to allow time for a robust discussion afterward.
	\item Special sesion and contributed talk speakers, please keep your talk to under \textbf{22 minutes} to allow for discussion and the set up of the next speaker.
\end{itemize}

To allow the smooth flow of talks, we ask all speakers to do the following:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Upload your slides using this Google form: \href{https://tinyurl.com/MCM2025Slides}{\nolinkurl{tinyurl.com/MCM2025Slides}}  \emph{at least two hours before your talk}.
	\item Uploads must be in pdf or ppt format.
	\item If you revise your slides, please submit an updated copy using the same link, but with a different file name.
	\item Slides will reside on a shared drive.
	\item When you upload your slides, you may specify whether or not you want conferees to be able to view them.
	\item As a safeguard, bring a copy of your slides on a USB drive.
	\item Go the room where they will present at least 10 minutes before the start of your session to familiarize yourself with the room and ensure that your slides are accessible.
\end{itemize}
Slides will be presented using the computers in the lecture rooms.  A conference assistant will help the speakers access the slides when giving their presentations.

\subsection{Session Chairs}

Thank you for ensuring that our conference runs smoothly.  Please
\begin{itemize}
	\item Arrive 15 minutes before the start of the session to familiarize yourself with the room and the technology set up.
	\item Introduce yourself to the conference assistant and the speakers in your session.
	\item Ensure that the conference assistant has downloaded all the talks from the shared drive to the comptuer in the room and that the files are readable.
	\item Before each talk, briefly introduce the speaker.
	\item Keep track of time and give reminders to speakers at the 5-minute and 2-minute marks.
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Aids will be provided.
		\item For sessions with fewer talks, keep to the schedule so that participants who may go between sessions will know the start and stop time.
		\item If there is an empty slot, feel free to facilitate an informal discussion if there is interest.
	\end{itemize}
	\item Ask the conference assistant to help if there are technical issues.
	\item When you upload your slides, you may specify whether or not you want them shared with conferees.
	\item As a safeguard, bring a copy of your slides on a USB drive.
\end{itemize}

The conference assistant will perform these steps but they are included for your reference.

\subsection{Nondiscrimination}

Illinois Institute of Technology (Illinois Tech) is committed to creating and maintaining a community dedicated to the advancement, application, and transmission of knowledge and creative endeavors through academic excellence, where all individuals who participate in University programs and activities can work and learn together in an atmosphere free of harassment, exploitation, or intimidation.

The University has policies, which prohibit discrimination, harassment, and sexual violence and address how to report such violations. These policies include the \href{https://webmaster.iit.edu/files/general-counsel/policies-and-procedures/procedure-p6-non-discrimination-policy.pdf}{Illinois Tech Policy on Non-Discrimination}, \href{https://webmaster.iit.edu/files/general-counsel/faculty-handbook/appendix_m.pdf}{Illinois Tech Policy on Sexual Harassment}, and
\href{https://www.iit.edu/hr/policies-and-procedures/employee-conduct/e2-sexual-and-other-forms-harassment}{Illinois Tech Policy Sexual and Other Forms of Harassment}.

The Illinois Tech Policy on Sexual and Other Forms of Harassment (Policy) addresses sexual violence, sexual harassment, and retaliation (“Prohibited Conduct”). This Policy outlines the University’s responsibilities and procedures related to Prohibited Conduct in order to ensure an equitable and inclusive education and employment environment free of sexual violence and sexual harassment. UCI Guidelines cover discrimination and harassment on the basis of race, color, national origin, religion, sex, gender, gender expression, gender identity, gender transition status, pregnancy, physical or mental disability, medical condition (cancer-related or genetic characteristics), genetic information (including family medical history), ancestry, marital status, age, sexual orientation, citizenship, or service in the uniformed services. The Policies applies to all University faculty, staff, and students (undergraduates, graduates, and professional students), and third parties. The Policy applies at all University campuses, University programs and activities.

Illinois Tech  will respond promptly and effectively to reports of Prohibited Conduct and will take appropriate action to prevent, stop, and remedy conduct violates the Policy.
Confidential resources, including services provided by the \href{https://www.iit.edu/shwc}{Illinois Tech Student Health and Wellness Center}
 and through the \href{https://portal.iit.edu/pages/human-resources-employee-assistance-program}{Employee Assistance Program}, are available to those who have experienced sexual harassment or sexual, and the latter can be reached 24 hours a day. A survivor can make use of confidential resources at any time, regardless of whether the event has been reported to the Title IX office or other authorities. For more information about Illinois Tech Student Health and Wellness and other support services at Illinois Tech see  Resources for the Student Health and Wellness Center.

Any person may report incidents of sexual harassment, discrimination or sexual violence to the campus Title IX office, which is named the
\href{https://www.iit.edu/title-ix}{Office of Title IX Compliance and HE Compliance}. Contact Title IX Office  by visiting  or by calling 312-567-5725.
Reports to law enforcement can be made to Illinois Tech Public Safety Department at 312-808-6363 for on-campus incidents or to the local department where the crime occurred. The Illinois Tech Public Safety Department or the Title IX Office can help determine which police department to contact. In an emergency, dial 911. Reports to Illinois Tech Public Safety Department, which is open 24 hours a day, can be made in person at 3424 S. State St., Chicago, Illinois.
